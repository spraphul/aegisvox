## 0:00 · Clinical conversations

Clinical conversations reveal identity through words and voices. But they also carry meaning, accent, pitch, pauses, breathing and hesitation. The challenge is to protect identity without losing useful context.

## 0:17 · Protecting only the words

Masking a transcript leaves the audio exposed. Muting identifiers can interrupt delivery, while the original voice remains.

## 0:27 · Changing only the voice

Changing only the voice leaves spoken names and locations in the conversation.

## 0:33 · Regenerating the conversation

Regenerating everything from text can sound fluent. But the original pauses, breaths and expression must be recreated.

## 0:44 · Consistent voice conversion

AegisVox separates voice identity from spoken identifiers. Each speaker receives one consistent surrogate voice. Voice conversion works from the original speech, with natural accent, pitch, pauses, breathing and hesitation as preservation goals.

## 1:02 · Document-consistent replacement

Identifying words receive consistent replacements in the source language. Repeated mentions use the same substitute.

## 1:11 · Complete-turn rendering

Speech editing uses the converted turn as a voice and style reference. Only complete turns with planned substitutions are rendered again. They rejoin the other converted turns, preserving the surrounding conversation.

## 1:26 · Lexical privacy

Then, three separate questions. Were identifiers found, were replacements spoken, and did any originals remain?

## 1:36 · Speaker linkability

Can an attacker still link the transformed voice to its original speaker?

## 1:42 · Clinical preservation

Did clinical meaning survive? What happened to corrections, speaker roles and useful acoustic cues?

## 1:51 · Separate objectives

Measure these outcomes separately, at the relevant stages. A different voice and fluent words are not enough.

## 2:00 · Governed use

Transformation is one layer of protection, not permission to share freely. Keep source audio within a protected workspace. Record its lineage and review residual risk. Hold uncertain outputs for review. Release approved audio only for an agreed purpose, with restricted access and continued monitoring.
