The architecture

Factorize the change.
Follow the evidence.

Changing words and changing a voice solve different problems. The implementation keeps those operations, their artifacts, and their measurements distinct.

01 / IDENTITY

A consistent voice per speaker

Profile speakers, retrieve distinct voice references, and convert all turns with SeedVC. The assignment follows the speaker, not the sentence.

All speaker turns
02 / LEXICAL

Edit the complete affected turn

Plan document-consistent replacements. Render the revised turn with its converted voice reference. Rejoin it with the other converted turns.

Selected complete turns
03 / EVIDENCE

Do not hide a tradeoff in one score

Inspect speaker linkage, replacement realization, retained identifiers, and clinical preservation at the stage where each was measured.
